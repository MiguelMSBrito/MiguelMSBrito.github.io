Why space, why ICARUS

I want to contribute to something that actually flies. ICARUS puts a Langmuir probe in orbit to study the ionosphere, and every part of it has to survive launch loads, the thermal swings of low Earth orbit and a tight memory budget on the on-board computer. That is exactly where I want to learn: structures and heat in my degree, careful C and C++ from my robotics work, and radio from the amateur licence I am preparing now.
