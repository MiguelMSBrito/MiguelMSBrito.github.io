About me

I went to school in Coimbra and moved to Porto this September to study Civil Engineering. At school I was always strongest in maths and physics, and over the last couple of years I also got into programming. This page puts together the things I have done so far, with some photos.

So far

  1. October 2026

    Amateur radio licence

    I am studying for the ANACOM Category 1 amateur radio exam in Porto. The first thing I want to try once I have the licence is sending a text message through QO-100, a satellite in geostationary orbit that radio amateurs can use from Portugal.

  2. September 2026

    Starting at FEUP

    I finished secondary school with an 18.5 average, with 20 in both Maths and Physics, and started Civil Engineering at the Faculty of Engineering in Porto. Mechanics was a big part of 12th grade physics, and a degree built around how structures carry loads felt like a natural next step.

  3. Spring 2026

    PmatE National Science Competition

    A team competition at the University of Aveiro. We won the Mathematics category, and each of us got a study grant from the university.

  4. March 2026

    BotOlympics: 2nd place

    Robotics competition at the University of Coimbra, final at Alma Shopping. Sponsors included Critical Software.

    Four of us from school entered the secondary school category. The robot had to get through a walled maze by itself and drop boxes at marked spots. I wrote the control code from scratch in C/C++: reading the sensors, driving the motors and deciding where to turn. It did not work the first time. Most of the three days went into running it on the track, seeing where it got stuck and changing the code before the next round. In the final we came second.

  5. 2024 to 2025

    Learning to program

    I wanted to learn programming properly, so I started lessons with a tutor. We began with C, then C++ and Java. A lot of the time went into memory and pointers, which turned out to be exactly what I needed a year later.

  6. 2024

    Maths Olympiad and Projeto Delfos

    I made it to the national finals of the Portuguese Mathematical Olympiad and joined Projeto Delfos, the olympiad training programme at the University of Coimbra. I kept going to Delfos until the end of 12th grade. Olympiad problems are mostly proofs, and they changed how I write a solution: I no longer skip steps because they look obvious.

  7. 2023

    End of 9th grade

    I was named a distinguished student at the end of 9th grade and went on a second Erasmus+ exchange, to Serbia. In September I started secondary school at Escola Secundária Avelar Brotero, in the science track.

  8. 2022

    Merit grant and a first Erasmus+ exchange

    The Parish of Coimbra gave me a school merit grant. The same year I went on an Erasmus+ exchange to Poland, the first time I had to work on a school project entirely in English with people I had never met.

  9. 2019

    Canguru Matemático

    My first maths competition. I was in 6th grade and came 3rd in my school. I kept entering, and in 2021 I came 1st in my school cluster.
